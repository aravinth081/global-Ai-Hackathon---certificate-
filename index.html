QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about GLOBAL AI HACKATHON 2026, submissions, certificates, and prizes.

What is GLOBAL AI HACKATHON 2026?

+
An international online hackathon hosted on Unstop by Innovation Hacks. It empowers students, developers, researchers, and innovators worldwide to build impactful solutions across Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, Agentic AI, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Healthcare, FinTech, and Smart Cities.

Who can participate and what are the team rules?

+
Participation is 100% online and open worldwide to students, developers, researchers, and tech enthusiasts. You can build as a solo innovator or in teams of 1 to 3 members. All registrations and team management are officially handled via Unstop.

How are certificates generated and verified?

+
Verified digital credentials are generated at print-ready 300 DPI resolution. Every certificate includes a tamper-proof cryptographic verification ID signed by Innovation Hacks, easily shareable on LinkedIn, resumes, and portfolios.

How do winners claim their prizes?

+
Winners share a total prize pool worth ₹30,000 across 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place champions. Official results are published on the Unstop portal and WhatsApp Community, followed by direct disbursement instructions from the organizing team.

What timezone is used for deadlines?

+
All schedules strictly follow Indian Standard Time (IST / UTC+05:30). Project submission window runs from 01 Sep 2026, 10:23 AM IST until the strict deadline of 10 Sep 2026, 11:23 PM IST on Unstop. Late submissions will not be accepted.

Where do we submit projects and get live announcements?

+
All final deliverables must be submitted directly through the Unstop platform before the deadline. For real-time updates and support, join our Official WhatsApp Channel or Community Group.
